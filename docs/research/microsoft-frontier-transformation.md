# Microsoft: Frontier Transformation

Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics-365/blog/business-leader/2026/03/09/a-new-way-of-working-is-taking-shape-frontier-transformation/

## What the article says

Microsoft describes a shift in work architecture built from three layers:

- an interface layer that moves to AI assistants
- an orchestration layer that agents use to run workflows
- an intelligence layer that consolidates information across structured and unstructured sources

The article also says business apps are changing shape. They still represent real business processes, but they are increasingly designed for agents and copilots to invoke.

## Main takeaways

- Work is shifting from screen navigation to intent and orchestration.
- Agents should sit between people and systems.
- Apps remain important, but as trusted services and systems of record.
- Shared intelligence across business data matters as much as the interface.
- A control plane becomes necessary once the agent count grows.

## Why it matters here

This is the clearest Microsoft match for the BC Gov architecture.

It supports three repo ideas at once:

- the front door should take intent, not form navigation
- specialist agents should orchestrate work
- the platform needs a knowledge layer that unifies structured and unstructured content

That last point matters for government knowledge brokerage. It is not enough to route a citizen to a form assistant. The router also needs to route to the right knowledge domain and the right retrieval mode, whether that is indexed content, RAG, semantic search, or a deeper research loop.
